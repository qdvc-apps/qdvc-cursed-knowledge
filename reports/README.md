# Cursed Knowledge reports

This folder receives reports of new cursed knowledge from other QDVC projects, both public and non-public. A maintainer later works through them and folds them into the main [Cursed Knowledge document](../README.md).

- [`inbox/`](inbox/) holds reports waiting to be reviewed. New reports go here, one gotcha per file.
- [`processed/`](processed/) holds reports that have been reviewed, each marked with its outcome. They stay here permanently, because entries from non-public projects link to them as their source.
- [`TEMPLATE.md`](TEMPLATE.md) is the template to copy when writing a report.

Writing a report? Read [`docs/WRITING-REPORTS.md`](../docs/WRITING-REPORTS.md) first. If your project isn't public, the rules there about what you may and may not include are not optional.

Maintaining this repo? See [`docs/MAINTENANCE.md`](../docs/MAINTENANCE.md).
